# Privacy Policy for Pulsar Music Player

**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026

Pulsar Music Player ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by Habitpluse as an offline, open-source desktop and mobile audio player. Your privacy is of paramount importance to us.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
Pulsar Music Player does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, audio files, usage analytics, or device identifiers. 

### 2. Local File Access
The application requires permission to access your local audio files and music folders solely for the purpose of indexing and playing your songs locally on your device. All playback, metadata reading, playlists, and equalizers operate 100% offline. No data ever leaves your device.

### 3. Network and Internet Usage
Pulsar Music Player does not track your listening habits or upload your files to any external cloud servers. Network access (if any) is restricted solely to standard application runtime dependencies or explicit user-initiated actions (such as checking for software updates).

### 4. Third-Party Services
Pulsar Music Player does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or user behavior analytics.

### 5. Children's Privacy
Because Pulsar Music Player does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children of any age.
